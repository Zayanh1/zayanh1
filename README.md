## 🌌 About Me
I'm a Computer Science student at the **University of Georgia (Morehead Honors College, Class of '28)** building highly polished, performant, and intelligent software. My development philosophy revolves around clean architecture, seamless user experiences (like magnetic cursors and scroll-reveals), and leveraging AI to automate the mundane. 

- 💻 **Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, Python, Supabase, Docker.
- 🎙️ **Current Focus:** Voice AI agents, full-stack relationship management tools, and personal knowledge systems.
- 💼 **Experience:** Previous Network Operations Intern @ GreenSky | Tech Support @ Forsyth County Schools | Data Destruction Specialist @ GCI.
- 📬 **Let's Connect:** [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zayanh1/) | [Portfolio](https://zayanh1.github.io)
